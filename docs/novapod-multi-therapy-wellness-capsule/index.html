Important Safety Information

For your safety, NovaPod sessions are not recommended for individuals with certain medical conditions or implanted devices. Please review the following carefully before booking.

Absolute Contraindications (Do Not Use)

  • Pacemakers or implanted electronic devices of any kind.
  • Active pregnancy.
  • Active cancer or currently undergoing cancer treatment, especially for tumors.
  • Uncontrolled heart conditions or severe cardiovascular instability.
  • Epilepsy or seizure disorders.

Situations Generally Acceptable (With Clearance Or Discretion)

  • Joint replacements such as artificial knees, hips, or surgical hardware.
  • Orthopedic implants that are non-electronic.
  • Healed surgical sites with no open wounds.
  • Stable, well-managed medical conditions

If you are unsure whether NovaPod is appropriate for you, please consult your healthcare provider before scheduling a session. Our staff is always happy to discuss general questions, but medical clearance is your responsibility.
