Reiki Master Attunement

Reiki Master Attunement

Deepen your Reiki practice with the Master Symbols. This attunement heightens your vibration level and allows you to share the gift of Reiki attunements with others.

Reiki stands as a sacred practice, demanding our utmost reverence and deepest respect to unlock its profound value. Beyond its ability to facilitate healing, a gift that holds immense significance, Reiki extends its benefits to provide guidance for our life's journey. Its boundless nature opens doors to constant growth, unfolding layers of potential that lie within us. The pursuit of Reiki unveils a path where joy, peace, and abundance steadily grow, offering not only personal blessings but also contributing to the healing our planet so desperately craves.

Initiates into Reiki often sense this expansive potential, propelling them to venture into the Advanced and Master levels. The allure of continued exploration and the awakening of one's limitless capabilities become a beacon for those on the Reiki path. May your journey into the depths of Reiki be one of discovery, transformation, and the realization of the profound interconnectedness between personal growth and global healing.

The desire to grow is inherent in simply being alive. As we look around ourselves and observe living things, we can clearly see that the one activity that all living things share is growth.

Everything that is alive grows. Because this is what living things do, one could even say that the purpose of life is to grow. Therefore, the desire to grow in ones Reiki potential is a natural expression of ones core essence and of life itself. If you feel this desire in your heart, honor and respect it. Doing so, will fulfill an innate need.

The joys of becoming a Reiki Master are many and you don't necessarily have to teach in order for the Master training to be useful. The additional healing energy, symbols, techniques and knowledge will add value to your healing abilities. Treating yourself, giving yourself and others healing attunements and treating others in person and at a distance will all be noticeably improved. The fact that you can pass Reiki on to friends and family is also a definite plus. Many take the Master training with just this in mind. However, if you ever decide to formally teach, you will be able to do so. As each person takes the Reiki Master training, and increases their personal vibration, this adds to the vibration of the whole planet! (by William Lee Rand)

Pre Requisite: Must have completed Reiki I and Reiki II (Reiki I and II Attunements acceptable with certificate from Certified Reiki Master)
