Reiki

Book an appointmentCall with questions

Reiki is a Japanese technique for stress reduction and relaxation that also promotes healing. It is administered by "laying on hands" and is based on the idea that an unseen "life force energy" flows through us and is what causes us to be alive. If one's "life force energy" is low, then we are more likely to get sick or feel stress, and if it is high, we are more capable of being happy and healthy.

The word Reiki comes from the Japanese word (Rei) which means “Universal Life” and (Ki) which means “Energy”. Reiki is not affiliated with any particular religion or religious practice. It is not massage nor is it based on belief or suggestion. It is a subtle and effective form of energywork using spiritually guided life force energy.

Reiki is the life energy that flows through all living things. Reiki Practitioners understand that everyone has the ability to connect with their own healing energy and use it to strengthen energy in themselves and help others. It is believed that a person’s “ki” or energy should be strong and free flowing. When this is true a person’s body and mind is in a positive state of health. When the energy becomes weak or blocked it could lead to symptoms of physical or emotional imbalance.

A Reiki session can help ease tension and stress and can help support the body to facilitate an environment for healing on all levels – physical, mental, and emotional. A session is pleasant and relaxing and is often utilized for one’s personal wellness. Many people use Reiki for in their own wellness practices. Reiki is not a cure for a disease or illness, but it may assist the body in creating an environment to facilitate healing. Reiki is a great tool to use as a complement to traditional medicine and is practiced in many hospitals and medical care settings.

Many times a client, after experiencing Reiki treatments, will wish to learn Reiki to work with this energy themselves by taking a Reiki I course. This can be beneficial as they may then use this stress reduction and relaxation tool anytime.

Please Note: Reiki is meant to be used as a complement to traditional care or as a personal relaxation or stress reducing tool. It is not a substitute for qualified medical or traditional care.

Gina L. Armstrong is a passionate and experienced Reiki Master Teacher with over two decades of dedication to energy healing. Gina was first attuned to Reiki I and Reiki II in 2001 and received additional attunements to these levels in 2002. She completed her formal studies in all levels of Reiki in 2004, earning her Usui Reiki Master certification.

Since 2008, Gina has been teaching all levels of Reiki and has shared her knowledge at several University of Wisconsin campuses. Her teaching style honors the sacred practice of Reiki and focuses on hands-on learning, fostering deeper understanding and confidence in her students. Gina believes that experiential practice is key to fully integrating Reiki into one’s personal and professional life.

Gina’s Reiki journey has been profoundly influenced by her studies with renowned teachers across the globe. She trained in the Peter Hess Sound Methods in Germany, studied color, sound, and vibration with Fabien Maman in Switzerland, and expanded her energy work under the guidance of Dr. John Wyrick. These diverse experiences enrich her Reiki practice, allowing her to blend complementary modalities into her teaching.

In her daily life, Gina embodies the principles of Reiki through self-Reiki and her work with clients. Her commitment to holding the practice sacred and her holistic approach to energy work inspire her students to develop their own deep connections to Reiki.

Whether you are new to Reiki or seeking to expand your practice, Gina offers a compassionate and dynamic learning environment where students can explore and grow in their understanding of this transformative healing art.
