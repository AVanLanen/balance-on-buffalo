Serving Manitowoc and the Lakeshore since 2002

Massage at Balance on Buffalo is rooted in presence, experience, and deep respect for the body's innate wisdom. We believe massage is not something done to the body, but a way of listening to it. No two bodies are the same, and neither are any two days, so each session is shaped with care around who you are when you walk in.

Every session is guided by a skilled, licensed therapist with a minimum of ten years of professional experience. To make choosing simple, we've refined our menu into three clear pathways.

Three pathways

Deep Rest Massage A hands-on, tool-free session rooted in skilled Swedish technique: long gliding strokes, steady kneading, and a lighter, thoughtful touch. Choose Deep Rest when you're overstimulated or worn down and what you need most is simplicity and the quiet intelligence of touch alone.

Attuned Massage A more focused, fully customized session guided by your body's cues and your therapist's cultivated expertise. Techniques, tools, and pacing are chosen intuitively as the session unfolds, which may mean cupping, hot stones, craniosacral work, or myofascial release. Choose Attuned when something specific needs attention, or when you'd rather hand the decisions to someone who has been doing this a long time.

Distinct Offerings Specialized, stand-alone sessions, each built around a single therapeutic intention: Craniosacral Therapy, Lymphatic Massage, Dosha Balance, and Full Stone Integration. Choose one when you already know exactly what you're after.

Enhancements

We've kept these simple on purpose. Rather than a long list of add-ons, there are two supportive options that can be included with either Deep Rest or Attuned: Deep Tissue Enhancement and Hemp-Derived CBD.

What every session includes

Some of the things that make the biggest difference are the ones you'd never think to ask for. Every massage table here is layered with a crystal-infused infrared BioMat woven with amethyst and tourmaline. Its low-EMF far-infrared warmth goes well beyond a standard table warmer, gently supporting circulation, muscle softening, and a calmer nervous system. Our hydraulic tables adjust effortlessly for comfort and alignment. And we use nourishing herbal massage oils that hydrate the skin and help the body settle.

All individual appointments also include time in our Quantum Breathing Room before or after your session, where zero-gravity recliners and infrared crystal support give you space to rest and let the work integrate.

None of these are upgrades. They're simply part of how we care for you.

Deep Rest Massage

45 • 60 • 90• 120 minutes

Deep Rest Massage is a soothing, hands-on experience rooted in the principles of Swedish massage. This offering is designed to help the body slow down, soften, and settle into a state of ease through gentle to moderate pressure and flowing, rhythmic touch.

Show more detailsShow fewer details

Using only the hands—without tools or added modalities—the therapist works with long, gliding strokes, supportive kneading, and calming movements that encourage circulation, relaxation, and nervous system balance. The simplicity of this approach allows the body to feel safe, supported, and deeply held.

Deep Rest is especially nourishing for those feeling overstimulated, fatigued, or in need of quiet care. It offers a sense of grounding and comfort, inviting the breath to deepen and the mind to rest.

For clients seeking a more expansive or fully customized experience—where the therapist may draw from additional tools, techniques, or intuitive guidance—we recommend exploring our Attuned Massage offering.

Attuned Massage

60 • 90 • 120 minutes

Attuned Massage is our most individualized and collaborative massage experience. This offering allows the therapist to draw fully from her training, experience, tools, and intuitive awareness to create a session that is uniquely shaped around you.

Show more detailsShow fewer details

Rather than choosing a specific modality in advance, you are invited to share your intention for the session—whether that’s relief, relaxation, grounding, or support through change. From there, the therapist listens not only to your words, but to your body, allowing the session to unfold in a way that is responsive and intentional.

Attuned sessions may include hands-on massage as well as the therapist’s preferred tools and specialized approaches, such as cupping, a variety of hot stones, craniosacral therapy, or myofascial release. These elements are chosen thoughtfully, based on what will best support your body in that moment.

All of our Licensed Massage Therapists bring a minimum of ten years of professional experience to their work. Over time, this experience cultivates a deep sense of discernment—an ability to feel when to soften, when to support, and when to introduce a specific technique or tool. Attuned Massage honors this wisdom and allows the therapist to practice at the highest level of their craft.

This offering is especially supportive if you are unsure what your body needs, prefer a therapist-led experience, or want the freedom for your session to be shaped intuitively and skillfully as it unfolds.
