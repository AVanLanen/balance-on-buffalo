QEWB: Quantum Energy Wellness Bed

Discover the Quantum Energy Wellness Bed (QEWB)

Sixty minutes on the QEWB. Warmth, sound, gentle bioelectric support, and a quiet energy field, all at once.

The Quantum Energy Wellness Bed is a titanium-lined wellness bed designed to help the body return to balance. It brings four gentle, non-invasive inputs together into one multi-sensory experience: long-infrared warmth, vibroacoustic sound, bioelectric support, and the bed's quantum field design. You lie down and let the bed do the work. What you feel is the nervous system calming, stress patterns letting go, and the body's own restorative processes coming forward once they're finally given the room.

People come to it for sleep, for pain and recovery, for stress they can't seem to put down, and for a reset when nothing else is landing. Here's what's actually happening while you're on it.

The titanium plate

This is what sets the QEWB apart from a heated mat with speakers. You lie on a full-length titanium plate paired with a bio-antenna design. Titanium is one of the few metals the body is completely at ease with (it's what surgical implants are made of), and it carries warmth, sound, and the body's own electrical activity evenly across the whole surface. Instead of a few warm spots or a speaker under your shoulders, everything reaches all of you at once. The plate is also the foundation for the bed's grounding and its energy field. Everything else is built on it.

How it works

The QEWB blends four modalities, each with its own history of use, into a single session.

Long-infrared warmth. A soothing, sauna-like warmth that penetrates the tissue rather than heating the air around you. Far infrared heat has been used as a healing modality for thousands of years. Here it relaxes muscles, improves circulation, supports detoxification and lymphatic flow, and invites the body into the kind of deep relaxation that's hard to reach on your own.

Vibroacoustic sound. Low-frequency sound and gentle vibration travel through the body, not just around it. You feel it as much as you hear it. The vibration stimulates cells, calms the nervous system, and encourages the brainwave states linked with relaxation and clarity. This is paired with the SoundLight Journey, a series of custom immersive tracks that build on one another. We'll start you on the first and move you forward as you return.

Bioelectric support. Your body is naturally electrical. The bed's titanium and bio-antenna design works gently with that electrical dimension, using low-level currents similar to the body's own signals to support cellular communication and repair responses. Most people don't feel this part at all. They notice it afterward.

Quantum field design. The titanium and bio-antenna architecture creates a subtle but measurable energy environment designed to promote coherence, which is simply the state where the body's systems work together more efficiently instead of pulling in different directions. It's the part that's hardest to put into words, and it's the piece most people point to when they describe the meditative, whole-body quality of a session. We won't overpromise here. We'll just say it's why people keep coming back.

Grounding

Like any device plugged into a standard three-prong outlet, the QEWB is grounded. Because the titanium plating runs the full length of your body, lying on the bed is a grounding experience in itself, similar to standing barefoot on the earth, with the current safely carried through the grounded outlet the bed is plugged into.

Why people choose QEWB sessions

Research into infrared, vibroacoustic, and microcurrent therapies has shown measurable benefits: reduced inflammation and swelling in both clinical and experimental models, decreased pain perception (by as much as 30 to 70 percent with repeated sessions), improved sleep quality and recovery from stress, and enhanced circulation and cellular activity that supports the body's natural repair cycles.

Results vary from person to person, and every session is different. But these are the things clients tell us about most often, and they line up with what the bed is designed to support.

Restful sleep. Deep relaxation and the conditions for more restorative rest.

Recovery and circulation. Circulation, muscular ease, and the body's natural recovery processes.

Comfort and ease. A more comfortable physical experience and greater ease of movement.

Cellular energy and vitality. Cellular function, energy production, strength, balance, and endurance.

Mental clarity and presence. Focus, cognition, and a calmer, more organized state of awareness.

Whole-person well-being. An integrated environment for physical, mental, emotional, and energetic well-being.

The QEWB doesn't do anything to you. It creates a safe, supportive environment where your body can do what it was designed to do: restore balance and vitality from within.

What a session looks like

Sessions are 60 minutes, and how you dress is up to you. Most people stay clothed: wear something soft with no metal, zippers, or sequins (cotton is ideal), and plan to remove jewelry and anything in your pockets. Some clients choose to fully disrobe so their skin is in direct contact with the titanium. Either way works; it's simply a matter of what feels right to you. The room is private and you'll have time to settle in before the session starts. Then rest, breathe, or drift off. Many people fall asleep.

Afterward, drink water and give yourself a few unhurried minutes before heading back out. If you'd like to stay in that state a little longer, you're welcome to rest in the Quantum Breathing Room and let the serenity carry on.

Bring your own pillow and blanket. This is our favorite tip. The biophotons the bed generates attach to fabric, so a pillow and blanket you bring from home carry a little of the session back with you. Depending on the material, that can last up to 24 hours. Natural fibers like cotton and wool hold it best.

Because the QEWB uses warmth and works with the body's electrical activity, it isn't for everyone. Please let us know before booking if you're pregnant, have a pacemaker or other implanted electrical device, or have a condition where heat isn't advised. The bed supports guests up to 300 lbs.
