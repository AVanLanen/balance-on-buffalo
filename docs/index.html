Where Sound, Light,
and Touch Align

Located in the heart of Manitowoc, Wisconsin, Balance on Buffalo is a holistic wellness center devoted to helping you slow down, restore balance, and reconnect with yourself. For over two decades, we’ve supported our community through thoughtful, experience-based care that integrates therapeutic touch with sound, light, breath, and frequency-based therapies.

Book an appointmentExplore our offerings

924 Buffalo Street · Manitowoc, Wisconsin

Our offerings include Therapeutic Massage, Halotherapy with Infrared Sauna, Peter Hess® Sound & Vibration Therapy, Reiki, and advanced wellness experiences such as the Harmonic Egg®, Quantum Energy Wellness Bed (QEWB®), and the NovaPod® Integrated Wellness System. Each modality is designed to support the nervous system, encourage deep relaxation, and create space for the body’s natural restorative processes.

At Balance on Buffalo, we believe wellness unfolds through presence, intention, and choice. Whether your path is through touch, sound, light, breath, or vibration, we invite you to explore what resonates—and rediscover harmony of mind, body, heart, and spirit.

Come experience the next evolution of wellness. Come home to yourself at Balance on Buffalo.
