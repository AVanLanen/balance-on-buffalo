Booking & Payment

Scheduling your appointment is easy through our online booking system by clicking Book Now.

To reserve your appointment, a valid credit card is required. Your card is securely stored and is not charged at the time of booking. It is used only if our 24-hour cancellation, late cancellation, no-show, or rescheduling policies apply.

Payment for your service is due at the time of your appointment. We gladly accept:

  • Credit or debit cards
  • HSA/FSA Cards
  • Cash
  • Gift cards
  • Chamber Bucks

At this time, we do not charge credit card processing fees.

If you have any questions or would like assistance scheduling your appointment, please call us at 920-684-8880. Our team is always happy to help.

Policies

Length Of Appointments

Kindly be aware that all massage therapy appointments are scheduled to include the booked session time, along with a minimum of 30 minutes in between. This buffer time enables our licensed massage therapist to switch rooms, reset both physically and mentally, and ensure they can provide you with the deserved attention during your full bodywork session. It's important to arrive on time, as late arrivals will result in a deduction from your scheduled time.

No Show & Cancellation Policy

We value your appointments at Balance On Buffalo – Therapeutic Massage Works. They are set aside exclusively for you. We understand that schedule changes can happen, so we kindly ask for a minimum of 24 hours' notice for cancellations.

When appointments are forgotten or canceled without adequate notice, it prevents us from filling that time slot and others from receiving services. To help with this, appointments are confirmed 48 hours beforehand via text or email, recognizing how easily appointments can slip one's mind, even if booked months prior. As these services are reserved just for you, a cancellation fee for the full amount of the scheduled service will be applied:

- Canceling or Rescheduling with less than 24-hour notice incurs a charge equal to 100% of the reserved service amount.

- "No shows" will also be charged 100% of the reserved service amount.

This cancellation policy permits us to inform waitlisted clients of any openings and ensures our team's schedules remain full, benefiting everyone. Your understanding and support of our policies are greatly appreciated.
