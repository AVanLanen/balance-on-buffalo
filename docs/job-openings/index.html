Licensed Massage Therapist

We are at capacity for Massage Therapists at this moment. Thank you for your Interest. We are always open to taking applications as things shift and change all the time.

Employee Position | Balance on Buffalo – Manitowoc, Wisconsin

Balance on Buffalo is an established wellness practice in Manitowoc, Wisconsin, offering therapeutic massage in an environment that values presence, professionalism, and client-centered care. This is a W-2 employee position for a Licensed Massage Therapist who practices with integrity, works independently, and does not require micromanagement.

This role is well suited for a therapist who values autonomy within clear structure, brings authenticity into their work, and maintains a strong focus on the client experience. We are seeking someone who takes ownership of their practice, honors professional standards, and approaches massage as a skilled, ethical profession.

Who You Are

  • Licensed Massage Therapist in the State of Wisconsin, in good standing
  • Self-directed, dependable, and comfortable working independently
  • Client-oriented, with the ability to be present, authentic, and attentive during sessions
  • Coachable and open to growth without the need for close supervision
  • Professional, punctual, and aligned with integrity-based practice
  • Experience preferred (3 years), though strong new graduates with maturity and self-awareness are welcome

What You’ll Do

  • Provide high-quality, client-centered massage therapy sessions that reflect the modalities you enjoy and excel in, while consistently offering a fantastic, well-grounded Swedish massage as the foundation of your work
  • Customize each session based on the client’s needs, goals, and comfort level
  • Communicate clearly with clients before and during sessions to ensure a safe, respectful, and effective experience
  • Offer simple self-care or home-care suggestions when appropriate
  • Maintain a clean, calm, and welcoming treatment space
  • Uphold professional boundaries, ethical standards, and scope of practice

Schedule & Compensation

  • Employee (W-2) position
  • Flexible yet consistent scheduling
  • Up to 20 hours of massage per week available
  • Competitive pay, established clientele, and therapists keep all gratuities
  • 5 paid holidays*
  • CEU reimbursement available*
